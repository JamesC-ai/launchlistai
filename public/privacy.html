Privacy

The free launch pack builder runs in your browser. It does not upload product notes automatically.

The paid pack download is also generated from the current browser inputs. Activation checks send only the activation code and product key to the license service; listing copy, maker comments, social drafts, screenshots, directory notes, and channel trackers are not sent by activation.

Activation codes may be stored in this browser's localStorage so the download can stay unlocked on the same device.

If you email a launch pack, only the email content you choose to send is transmitted to support@pagecheckai.com.
